Fixed by selecting ESP32S3 Dev Module and installing core 3.3.12 (the pinned version). The board's BOOT and RST buttons can't be reached (it's mounted upside down on the breadboard), so INC-01 was uploaded by holding the doorbell button to keep the board awake, and reset by unplugging and replugging USB.

Bench notes (2026-11-12, Indoor Ringer): the new SuperMini boot-looped on its factory firmware; recovered with BOOT held while plugging in (Discover D2), then RST after upload. The Ringer has no button of its own in the finished design as Ron describes it; a temporary button on GPIO2 was wired for RT-03 (option A: INC-02 unchanged). Whether the finished Ringer keeps a button is still open.
