# Increment card INC-02: the Indoor Ringer rings

- **Backlog item:** rank 2 (Decide §5): the Indoor Ringer plays the built-in sound on a ring message and on
  its own test button.
- **Requirement:** the Indoor Ringer plays the built-in sound (the factory default, DL-028) at power-on,
  once for each press of its test button, and once for each ring message. It's on USB power and stays
  awake. A ring that arrives while it's chiming is ignored. It's louder than the Doorbell (GAIN 15 dB).
  Decisions: DL-027.
- **Increment type:** behavior change (new firmware: `firmware/indoor-ringer/`).
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  1. [Local] Power-on plays the built-in sound once. (AC1)
  2. [Local] The sound played is the built-in sound the logic was given. (AC2)
  3. [Local] Each test-button press plays it once. (AC3)
  4. [Local] Holding the button gives one sound, not a repeat. (AC4)
  5. [Local] Contact bounce on press and release counts as one press. (AC5)
  6. [Local] A glitch shorter than the debounce time (30 ms) isn't a press. (AC6)
  7. [Local] A ring message plays it once. (AC7)
  8. [Local] A ring that arrived while a sound was playing is ignored; a later one plays. (AC8)
  9. [Local] If playback fails, the Ringer keeps working. (AC9)
  10. [Gate] The Ringer builds with its pinned profile and stays within B-05 (≤ 1,572,864 bytes); its
      sound header is generated from `sounds/builtin_dingdong.wav` (A-06).
  11. [Real] RT-03: power-on chime, 10 presses = 10 chimes, a 6 s hold = one chime, a press during a chime
      is ignored, and louder than the Doorbell by ear.
- **Simulated tier:** none (Wokwi doesn't implement I2S on the ESP32-S3).
- **Protected behavior:** all absolutes; baseline 1 (the Doorbell's INC-01 checks and tests).
- **Risks:** the I2S wrapper and the sound header are copies of the Doorbell's (moving shared parts into
  `firmware/common/` is a separate, later decision, not this increment). The ring-message input exists
  only as an interface until rank 3. Louder by ear is a judgment, not a measurement.
- **Files in scope:**
  - `firmware/indoor-ringer/src/logic/ports.h`, `src/logic/ring/ringer.h` (declarations, lock commit;
    generation may change the class's private section, not its public interface)
  - `firmware/indoor-ringer/src/logic/ring/ringer.cpp` (lock-commit stub; generation replaces it)
  - `firmware/indoor-ringer/src/hw/` (I2S playback; the test button; the clock; a ring input that never
    reports a ring until rank 3)
  - `firmware/indoor-ringer/src/sounds/builtin_dingdong.h` (generated by `tools/wav2header.py`)
  - `firmware/indoor-ringer/indoor-ringer.ino` (wiring only)
  - `tests/local/locked/test_inc02_ringer.cpp`; `tests/real/RT-03-ringer-rings.md`
- **Fits the mold?** Yes, after the wall change in this branch (Ringer build profile, gate step, B-05; DL-027).
- **Checks locked:** lock commit on branch `inc/02-ringer-rings`. Lock run: all 9 new Local checks compiled,
  ran and failed on their assertions against the stub; none passed, so none was reclassified.
- **Real-tier procedures required:** RT-03.
